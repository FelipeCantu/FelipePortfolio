# Notes: a-comprehensive-guide-to-becoming-a-successful-freelancer-on-fiverr

Researched November 22, 2026. Fiverr changes policies often; recheck the help center links before you publish.

## (a) Claims to double-check

1. **Buyer Requests → Briefs → Mira.** Fiverr's Briefs help article now says "Mira AI Headhunter has fully replaced the Briefs feature." The details about Mira (an AI drafts the job description and messages matched freelancers; no outreach to freelancers for budgets under $250; reply within 24 hours to protect your response rate) come from "Finding talent with Mira." This is new and changing fast, so check it right before publishing. Sources: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415608857745 and https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/48756531483537-Finding-talent-with-Mira
2. **Off-platform rules.** Paying or doing business off Fiverr is not allowed. Third-party tools are allowed only after an order is placed, and those conversations should be summarized back on Fiverr. Don't share passwords. Don't use remote-control tools like TeamViewer or AnyDesk. The penalty is a warning, then possible suspension. Building a website is named as a legitimate case that needs some outside access. Sources: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/12792122691601 and Fiverr's community blog post "Taking business off-platform" (https://community.fiverr.com/public/blogs/taking-business-off-platform-2025-06-02). I could not load the full Terms of Service or the Community Standards off-platform page (https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/38728907371665), so two things are **unverified**: whether a Fiverr client can later hire you directly, and whether a personal website link is allowed anywhere on your profile or portfolio. That's why the article tells readers to keep Fiverr clients on Fiverr.
3. **Fee and clearance.** Freelancers receive 80% of the cleared payment, and the funds clear in 14 days. Top Rated, Fiverr Pro and Seller Plus get a shorter period, and the milestones article says 7 days. The client has 3 days to respond to a delivery before it auto-completes. Sources: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/34069565843985, https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011421198-FAQs-for-freelancers, https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010560178-Working-with-Milestones. **Unverified:** whether the 20% also applies to tips and extras, and the client-side service fee. The article doesn't state either.
4. **Seller levels.** The six metrics and thresholds for each level, the daily evaluation, response rate (replies within 24 hours over 90 days), the 2-year rating window, the 30-day grace period and the extra review for Top Rated. Source: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010560118-Fiverr-s-new-level-system
5. **Milestones.** Available only in some subcategories, website design included. The basic package must be at least $100 and each milestone at least $50. Gigs allow 2 to 5 milestones and custom offers up to 6. Revisions are set per milestone. Source: Working with Milestones (link above). Check that the web development subcategories (not only "Website Design") are eligible.
6. **Fiverr Pro.** Freelancers apply and are vetted. No membership fee, standard commission, and it depends on your category being open. Source: https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/29453001296913-How-to-become-a-Fiverr-Pro-freelancer. "Aimed at business clients with bigger budgets" is a general description, not a figure.
7. **Gig features I did not check against the help center this session:** three packages per gig, gig extras, requirements questions, the availability setting, and responding publicly to reviews. They exist as far as I know, but they're unverified. Subscription orders exist (help article "Subscription orders for freelancers"), but I didn't read the details.
8. **Reviews.** "Don't offer discounts or extras in exchange for a review" matches Fiverr's general policy against review manipulation, but I didn't read the exact policy text. Unverified.
9. **Taxes.** I deliberately gave no thresholds and no 1099-K figures, because US reporting thresholds have changed recently. The article only says to set money aside and talk to an accountant.
10. **Felipe facts used:** only his client portal (proposals, e-signed agreements, Stripe invoices, file approvals, previews, support requests). I made no claim that he has sold on Fiverr.

## (b) Dropped from the original, and why

- **All emoji and generic motivation.** Your style guide calls for removing them.
- **Buyer Requests advice.** The feature no longer exists. I replaced it with how Briefs and Mira work.
- **Generic "promote your gig on social media".** I folded it into building your own channel. Sharing a gig link is fine, but the bigger lesson is not to rely on the marketplace.
- **Non-developer niche examples.** The audience was narrowed to developers, as you asked.
- **Any implied tactic of moving Fiverr clients to your own site or payments.** It breaks Fiverr's rules. The article frames your own site as a separate channel for clients who don't come through Fiverr.
