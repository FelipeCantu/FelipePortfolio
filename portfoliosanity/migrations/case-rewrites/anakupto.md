Anakupto is a Three.js website for music artists. Instead of a standard landing page, fans get a 3D world they can explore in real time, right in the browser.

## Where it stands

The project is in progress. The live build at [anakupto.vercel.app](https://anakupto.vercel.app) opens on the "Transcend the screen" headline over a glossy black 3D knot on a starfield.

## Built with

- **Three.js** for the 3D scene.
- **WebGL** to render it in the browser, with no plugins or apps to install.
